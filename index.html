How it works

Not a job board. A matching platform.

We pair athletes and clubs on skill, coaching style, personality, housing needs, and culture — not just a résumé and a prayer.

  1. 01

    Build your profile

    Athletes add playing level, coaching experience, availability, references, and a short video intro. Clubs post with full context.

  2. 02

    Get matched

    We surface the best fits on sport, skill, personality, housing needs, and culture. No cold applying into the void.

  3. 03

    Connect & confirm

    Clubs review ranked applicants, message athletes directly, and confirm placements in one place. No email chains.

  4. 04

    Build reputation

    After the season both sides review each other. Your reputation travels with you, compounding every summer.
